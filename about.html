About Savanna Brew Café

More than a coffee.....it's a life sytle.

Our Story

Savanna Brew Café opened in 2019 with one small espresso machine and a big dream. Our founder, Amina Otieno, grew up on her grandparents' coffee farm in Nyeri, where she developed a deep appreciation for Kenyan coffee.

Today, we bring that passion to Westlands, Nairobi, by serving quality coffee made from carefully selected Kenyan coffee beans.

We believe in supporting local farmers, using eco-friendly packaging and creating a welcoming environment for everyone. We also organise monthly community events that bring people together.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do.

Quality

We use freshly roasted, single-origin Kenyan coffee beans to give our customers an excellent coffee experience.

Community

We believe in offering fair prices to farmers and providing a welcoming space for everyone.

Sustainability

We support the environment through compostable cups and zero-waste pastries.

"My dream was to celebrate Kenyan coffee and create a welcoming place where people can connect, relax and enjoy every moment."

— Amina Otieno, Founder
